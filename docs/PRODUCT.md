# SKB product direction

The founder participates, competes, judges, teaches, and shares resources. The product should make those roles reinforce each other rather than reduce debate to an automated win/loss score.

## First release

A broader speech and debate community can use free resources, short AI-authored video lessons, a full PF practice room, case preparation, and human-entered judging feedback. The free library includes entry points for speech, impromptu, extemporaneous speaking, original oratory, PF, BP, MUN, and judging. PF is the first fully simulated format.

The core loop is **learn a technique → prepare → practice → receive a ballot → choose the next exercise → contribute what helped**. Video lessons have transcripts and captions. Examples are clearly separated from verified evidence.

## Next product increments

1. **Founder teaching voice:** Review and expand the six scripts, record demonstrations, add real annotated speeches with permission, and introduce longer course sequences. Replace synthetic narration where human demonstration adds value. Add bilingual teaching based on actual demand.
2. **Adaptive practice:** Connect a funded model provider through server-side calls. Introduce a responsive opponent, transcript-grounded feedback, evidence provenance, clear data consent, per-user usage caps, and model/cost logging. Test feedback quality against human judges before promising assessment reliability.
3. **Teaching community:** Migrate the original tournament/workshop and volunteer components once their data is available. Add teacher-led assignments and invitations using a verified identity model. Publish only real event listings, and implement editorial policies consistently across resources and discussions.
4. **Advanced paid prep:** Define the actual value first: deeper case review, tournament-specific prep organization, advanced drills, or a coaching workflow. Add payments and entitlements only once pricing, billing identity, refunds, and operating costs are decided. Keep foundational resources and introductory courses free.
5. **Independent commercial app:** Add a public domain and a suitable external identity provider, mobile/PWA packaging, database backup/export operations, support ownership, and a production monitoring routine. The current Sites deployment is a usable hosted first release, not an app-store package or a complete paid launch.

## Social impact

Measure access and learning rather than make unsupported impact claims: successful free lesson completions, completed practice rounds, teacher use, contributor participation, and rubric-based improvement in a voluntary pilot. Do not count account creation as skill improvement. Keep exports available and preserve a free path for students who cannot pay.

## Commercial decision boundaries

No prices, fabricated users, testimonials, course credentials, event dates, or revenue projections are assumed. An optional paid advanced-preparation tier is a direction, not an active product in this release. Live AI costs and output quality must be measured before unlimited usage is sold.
